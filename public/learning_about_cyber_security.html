Learning About Cybersecurity

A complete beginner's roadmap from zero knowledge to job-ready, with free resources, key certifications, and industry frameworks.

Phase 01

🖥️
How Computers Work

Foundational
🌐
Networking Basics
TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP/HTTPS, firewalls, subnetting. Understanding how data moves is non-negotiable in security.
Critical
🐧

Essential
🔑
Security Core Concepts
CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability), authentication vs. authorization, encryption basics.
Must Know

04
Phase 04
Learn Industry Frameworks
Weeks 16 to 28  |  Intermediate
NIST
National Institute of Standards and Technology
The gold standard for organizational security policy. The NIST CSF (Cybersecurity Framework) covers Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover. Required knowledge for any SOC or compliance role.
MITRE ATT&CK
Adversarial Tactics, Techniques and Common Knowledge
A real-world knowledge base of how attackers actually behave. Used by SOC analysts to detect threats and map attack patterns. Essential for threat hunting and incident response.
OWASP
Open Worldwide Application Security Project
The OWASP Top 10 is the definitive list of critical web application vulnerabilities (SQL injection, XSS, etc.). Free resource. Critical for anyone going into AppSec or pentesting.
ISO 27001
International Organization for Standardization
International standard for information security management systems (ISMS). Widely referenced in enterprise environments and vendor assessments. Good to know for GRC roles.
Cyber Kill Chain
Lockheed Martin Framework
Maps the 7 stages of a cyberattack from reconnaissance to exfiltration. Helps defenders understand where to break the chain and stop attackers before damage occurs.
🗺️ You do not need to memorize these frameworks. You need to understand what each one is for and when to apply it. Employers want to know you speak the language.
05
Phase 05
Break In and Get Hired
Month 6 onward  |  Job Ready
📁
Build a Portfolio
Document your TryHackMe and HTB progress. Write up home lab projects. GitHub everything. Show work, not just credentials.
Differentiator
💼
Entry Level Roles
Target SOC Analyst Tier 1, IT Support with security duties, Security Operations Intern, or Junior Penetration Tester.
Target Roles
🔗
LinkedIn + Networking
Optimize your profile with certifications. Follow CISA, SANS Institute, and Krebs on Security. Comment on posts and be visible.
Visibility
📰
Stay Current
Read Krebs on Security, The Hacker News, and Dark Reading weekly. Subscribe to CISA advisories. Security moves fast.
Ongoing
